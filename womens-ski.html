3-Day Women's Ski Mountaineering Seminar

Plan My Trip
Overview

As one of the top ski mountaineering peaks in the world, Mt. Shasta is the perfect place to hone in your ski mountaineering skills. And to do so with the support of an all female team will make this a fun and informative adventure!

This 3 day ski and board expedition will explore what Mt. Shasta is famous for; classic spring skiing and big mountain ski lines. We will climb and ski either the Avalanche Gulch or West Face route, depending on current conditions and weather. Guided by our professional female staff, this course is a unique opportunity for women to develop and hone a ski mountaineering skill set with a crew of exceptionally supportive and professional ladies.

Ski Mountaineering instruction includes:

  • Ice axe and crampon use
  • Ice axe self-arrest
  • Snow climbing and skinning technique
  • Basic rope travel
  • Avalanche Awareness
  • Transceiver Practice

In this seminar, we aim to create personal connections with Mt. Shasta and the surrounding land and building lasting connections to your new snow sliding community.

We have logged over 45 years of experience guiding on Mount Shasta and are the original company for this special mountain. We know Shasta better than anyone and aim to provide a professional and positive experience for all climbers.

Day by Day

Planned Itinerary

Day 1

  1. 8:00amMeet guides and group at SMG shop 305 Chestnut St Mt. Shasta. We will conduct a thorough gear check and distribute group equipment. Plan to add 8-10 pounds of group gear to your personal kit; this includes food and tents.
  2. 10:00amDrive to the Bunny Flat trailhead (6,950’) for a group briefing and start the approach to base camp, typically treeline at 8,000’ or higher based on snow conditions
  3. 2:00pmAt base camp your group will set up camp, snack on trail food and prepare for a light ski tour or afternoon skills session
  4. 5:00pmReturn to camp for an early dinner and evening briefing.

Day 2

  1. 7:00amWake-up, hot breakfast, and pack for day tour and skills clinics.
  2. 9:00am-2:00pmWe will tour on the lower flanks of the mountain looking for the best ski terrain and conditions while refining our ski mountaineering techniques
  3. 2:00-5:00pmWe will continue with an additional skills session and advanced training and review at camp while we rehydrate and prepare for the big days ahead.
  4. 5:00-7:00pmDinner and climb briefing

Day 3

  1. Shasta SkiAlpine Start | Depending on our weather and route conditions we will select terrain where we can have the best snow conditions while climbing and skiing on Mt. Shasta. With over 5,000’ of gain from camp, there are big options for us on our summit day. Depending on our team abilities, weather, and route conditions we will begin our climb between 2:00-3:00am
  2. Turn around timeFor safety purposes, our turn around time on the mountain may be as early as 11:00-12:00pm. We use these time frames to avoid hazards as well as optimize ski conditions. Sometimes we may delay our descent to wait for softening of the snow surface, every day is different and we’ll use our experience to judge best conditions.
  3. Back at base camp the team will pack camp and head back to the trailhead. We’re typically back to the trailhead between 4:00-5:00p,

Itinerary subject to change & the summit of Mount Shasta is subject to safe weather and snow conditions. We may chose to alter the itinerary to best serve the safety and goals of the group

For lodging in the town of Mount Shasta before and after your trip we recommend our partners at Summit Lofts and Cold Creek Inn. They offer a discount to our climbers and guests.
