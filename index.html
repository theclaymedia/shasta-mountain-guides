Why Shasta Mountain Guides

This is our home.

Shasta Mountain Guides is the original climbing guide service on Mt. Shasta and leads the highest quality expeditions and courses on the mountain. For over 40 years we have shared this remarkable experience offering an unmatched level of service on Mount Shasta. This is our home and we have the ultimate respect for the mountain, the community, and our family of guides and staff who work diligently to help you achieve your goals. We are local professionals committed to your safety and enjoyment. This climb is ideal for new and experienced mountaineers and with the proper training and preparation, the summit is a very attainable goal. Be ready for the trip of a lifetime!

We are taking reservations for the 2026 climbing season, our 43rd year leading climbs on Mt. Shasta.

Meet Your Guides

The people who will get you to the top.

SMG was founded in the late 1970s by Michael Zanger. In 2003 longtime guides Chris and Jenn Carr took over and have grown the team to a seasonal staff of over 30. Our common bond is a pure passion for being in the mountains and sharing that with you. Between us we have thousands of Shasta summits and we have explored every route in every season.

You will know your guide before you clip in. Every trip is led by a certified professional who lives and works on this mountain, not a contractor flown in for the weekend.

CC

Chris Carr

Co-Owner · AIARE III, AMGA Alpine & Ski

Living and climbing in Mt. Shasta since 1995 with over 180 summits and countless ski descents. Chris runs operations and you will still find him on the mountain checking conditions. Lives in Mt. Shasta

JC

Jenn Carr

Co-Owner · EMT, WFR, AIARE III

A California native who started guiding on Shasta in 1998 with over 50 summits. Jenn runs the daily operations and is often the first voice you hear when you reach out. Lives in Mt. Shasta

JK

Jenna Kane

Guide · AIARE I, OEC, WFR

Born and raised in Mt. Shasta City, Jenna patrols at Mt. Shasta Ski Park in winter and coaches the Mt. Shasta High School ski and snowboard team. Born here

NC

Nick Caselli

Senior Guide · AAA I, EMT, OEC

Twenty years of rock, ice, alpine and ski routes across North America. Former Patrol Director at Mt. Shasta Ski Park and a firefighter EMT in the off-season. Mt. Shasta local

DB

Dane Brinkley

Senior Guide · AIARE III Instructor, WFR

Over 200 Shasta summits, often on skis, plus three Denali expeditions. Dane guides year round on every major route and teaches our AIARE avalanche courses.

CG

Lead Guide · WEMT, NREMT

Cascade volcano climber and skier who has skied from the summit of Denali. Casey patrols at Mt. Shasta Ski Park and believes the most important piece of gear is a positive mental attitude. Mt. Shasta local

Meet the whole team
Rooted in Mt. Shasta

We don't drive in for the season. We live here.

Other outfits guide Shasta from out of the area. Our office is on Chestnut Street, our guides patrol the local ski park, coach the local kids, and read this mountain every single day. That local knowledge is what keeps you safe and gets you home with a story.

  • On the mountain dailyLive conditions from guides who were up there this morning, not a forecast from another state.
  • Sponsoring local youthWe back local youth ski, snowboard and climbing teams because the next generation of mountain people starts here.
  • 305 Chestnut StreetStop by the shop in town before your trip. Real people, real gear, real answers.
