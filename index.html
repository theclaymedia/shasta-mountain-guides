Why Shasta Mountain Guides

This is our home.

Shasta Mountain Guides is the original climbing guide service on Mt. Shasta and leads the highest quality expeditions and courses on the mountain. For over 45 years we have shared this remarkable experience offering an unmatched level of service on Mount Shasta. This is our home and we have the ultimate respect for the mountain, the community, and our family of guides and staff who work diligently to help you achieve your goals. We are local professionals committed to your safety and enjoyment. This climb is ideal for new and experienced mountaineers and with the proper training and preparation, the summit is a very attainable goal. Be ready for the trip of a lifetime!

We are taking reservations for the 2027 climbing season, our 45th year leading climbs on Mt. Shasta.

Meet Your Guides

The people who will get you to the top.

SMG was founded in the late 1970s by Michael Zanger. In 2003 longtime guides Chris and Jenn Carr took over and have grown the team to a seasonal staff of over 30. Our common bond is a pure passion for being in the mountains and sharing that with you. Between us we have thousands of Shasta summits and we have explored every route in every season.

You will know your guide before you clip in. Every trip is led by a certified professional who lives and works on this mountain, not a contractor flown in for the weekend.

Chris Carr

Co-Owner · AIARE III, AMGA Alpine & Ski

Living and climbing in Mt. Shasta since 1995 with over 180 summits and countless ski descents. Chris runs operations and you will still find him on the mountain checking conditions. Lives in Mt. Shasta

A California native who started guiding on Shasta in 1998 with over 50 summits. Jenn runs the daily operations and is often the first voice you hear when you reach out. Lives in Mt. Shasta

Twenty-five years guiding peaks from the Alps to Antarctica and close to 150 Shasta summits. Rich runs our guide training, special events and avalanche seminars.

Meet the whole team
Rooted in Mt. Shasta

We don't drive in for the season. We live here.

Other outfits guide Shasta from out of the area. Our office is on Chestnut Street, our guides patrol the local ski park, coach the local kids, and read this mountain every single day. That local knowledge is what keeps you safe and gets you home with a story.

  • On the mountain dailyLive conditions from guides who were up there this morning, not a forecast from another state.
  • Sponsoring local youthProud sponsors of Mt. Shasta Youth Soccer, the Mt. Shasta Ski Team and the Mt. Shasta Swim Team, because the next generation of mountain people starts here.
  • 305 Chestnut StreetStop by the shop in town before your trip. Real people, real gear, real answers.
