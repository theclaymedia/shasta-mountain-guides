Overview

Ready to climb Mt. Shasta? Shasta Mountain Guides is the original guide service on Mt. Shasta and leads the highest quality trips and expeditions on the mountain. For over 45 years we have shared this remarkable experience and are excited to help you plan an adventure.

This climb is ideal for new and experienced mountaineers. With the proper training and preparation the summit is a very attainable goal. Be ready for the trip of a lifetime.

Current status: We have posted our dates and programs for 2027 and are hoping for a deep snowpack on Mt. Shasta and an excellent year for climbing and skiing on the volcano.

  • SeasonLate April to mid August

What's Included

We carry the mountain stuff. You carry you.

  • Professional instruction and mountain guiding (1:3 guide-to-client ratio on summit day)
  • Breakfast and dinner while in the backcountry
  • Group camping equipment: tents, stoves and kitchen gear
  • Ropes, harnesses, drinking water, hot drinks, emergency first aid and communication equipment
  • Mt. Shasta summit passes and permits

Boots are not included, but rentals are available in town at The Fifth Season.
