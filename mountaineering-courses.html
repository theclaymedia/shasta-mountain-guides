Mountaineering Courses

Build a solid foundation of mountain skills on one of California’s largest glaciers, then put them to work on a summit climb.

Plan My Trip
Overview

As a major volcanic peak in the Cascade Range, Mt. Shasta is an ideal place to learn and develop the key skills of a proficient mountaineer and alpine climber.

Our four and five-day seminars build a solid foundation of mountain skills while making a summit climb on one of California’s largest glaciers. Our guides are highly skilled climbers and teachers, eager to share their experience with you.

  • SeasonMay through August
  • Course length1 to 5 days
  • ExperienceNone required for Day Class and Glacier I
  • From$275 for the Day Class

What's Included

We carry the mountain stuff. You carry you.

  • Professional instruction and mountain guiding (1:3 guide-to-client ratio on summit day)
  • Breakfast and dinner while in the backcountry
  • Group camping equipment: tents, stoves and kitchen gear
  • Ropes, harnesses, drinking water, hot drinks, emergency first aid and communication equipment
  • Mt. Shasta summit passes and permits

Boots are not included, but rentals are available in town at The Fifth Season.

Dates and availability

Scheduled departures run through the season and private dates are available on request. Tell us your window and we will match you to a trip.
