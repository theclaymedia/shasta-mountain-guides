Overview

While we offer dozens of classes, trips and seminars, sometimes you need something a little different. Or something more personal. Or to accommodate a special need.

We are happy to oblige. We create custom and private trips all the time: summit climbs, courses, day hikes, ski tours and rock climbs. Basically, whatever you want. Tell us what you are looking for and we will build it.

What's Included

We carry the mountain stuff. You carry you.

  • Professional instruction and mountain guiding (1:3 guide-to-client ratio on summit day)
  • Breakfast and dinner while in the backcountry
  • Group camping equipment: tents, stoves and kitchen gear
  • Ropes, harnesses, drinking water, hot drinks, emergency first aid and communication equipment
  • Mt. Shasta summit passes and permits

Boots are not included, but rentals are available in town at The Fifth Season.

Dates and availability

Scheduled departures run through the season and private dates are available on request. Tell us your window and we will match you to a trip.
