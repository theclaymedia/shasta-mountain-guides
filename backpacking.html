Overview

Our backpacking and trekking trips are a great way to explore Mt. Shasta and the Shasta-Trinity National Forest. Our Mt. Shasta Basecamp Treks are non-technical hikes to the upper mountain without the commitment of a summit climb. The basecamps are spectacular: fresh snowmelt drinking water, amazing views, perched at the toe of Shasta’s glaciers.

Our PCT trips cover some of the most breathtaking miles of the Pacific Crest Trail in three days. Camp beside alpine lakes, eat perfectly curated meals, and learn from seasoned experts how to backpack in the wilderness.
