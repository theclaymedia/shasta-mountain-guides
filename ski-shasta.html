Backcountry Skiing on Mt. Shasta

Over 7,000 feet of vertical, a deep maritime snowpack and reliable California weather. Every turn is earned.

Plan My Trip
Overview

An icon and mecca for backcountry snow riders, Mt. Shasta has been called one of the top five ski mountaineering peaks in the world. The backcountry skiing and riding are legendary in the Cascades and Shasta is the crown jewel.

With skins and ski crampons, each turn is earned. We offer single and multi-day tours, seminars and summit descents for skiers and splitboarders.

  • SeasonDecember through June
  • Trip length1 to 5 days
  • AbilityAdvanced downhill for descents; intermediate for intro tours
  • From$495 for a day tour
