Overview

Perhaps the best 5.6 in California, this incredible 5-pitch climb, pioneered by SMG Founder Michael Zanger with Himalayan Legend Chris Bonington, climbs the imposing 800′ east face of Mt. Hubris aka “The Ogre” in Castle Crags State Park. With everything from slabs, face, cracks, and a dramatic and exposed alpine ridge finish, this climb is sure to satisfy everyone from new to experienced climbers.

Previous multi pitch experience is necessary and we can help you prepare for this ascent with a climbing skills day in advance.

On this climb, we aim to create personal connections with the surrounding land and sustaining connections to your new climbing community.

Experience & Fitness

Is this trip right for you?

  • Experience Required. For climbers with previous traditional climbing experience.
  • Ages 16 and up.
  • Requires previous multi-pitch climbing experience or skills day prior to the climb.
  • Castle Crags climbs require a strenuous 2-3 hour uphill approach hike to access the rock and climbers should be in good physical condition.
