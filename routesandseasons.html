Routes and Seasons

Where we climb, and when. The route follows the snow: the south side in spring, the north and east sides through summer.

Seasons

When we climb where.

Late April to early June

South & Southwest Side

From late April through early June we are typically climbing on the South and Southwest side, utilizing the Avalanche Gulch, the West Face, or Casaval Ridge routes. There are options for both new and experienced mountaineers.

Avalanche Gulch, 3-dayAvalanche Gulch, 2-dayWest FaceCasaval Ridge
June to early September

North & East Side

From June through early September, we climb the Hotlum-Bolam Ridge, Hotlum-Wintun Ridge, the Hotlum Glacier, or Clear Creek routes on the North and east aspects of Mt. Shasta. These are suitable options for new and experienced mountain climbers, especially those looking for additional and more advanced training.
